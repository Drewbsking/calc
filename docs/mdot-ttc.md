# MDOT TTC Typical Selector

The Work Zones card opens `calculators/mdot-ttc/`. Users browse all individual MDOT construction, maintenance, and survey traffic typicals, keep a separate project selection, download an editable Word table for Maintenance of Traffic (MOT), combine selected MDOT sheets into one PDF for GI and the preliminary spec book, or package individual PDFs in a ZIP for final submittal to MDOT Specs and Estimates. The tool uses the existing static site and makes no runtime requests to MDOT for exports.

## Catalog sources and meaning

The catalog contains **136 individual typicals: 88 construction, 32 maintenance, and 16 survey**. Membership comes from all three official MDOT PDF catalogs, so future refreshes include newly listed typicals even when the workbook has no corresponding row. Combined sets, CAD files, and sign-calculation spreadsheets are excluded to avoid duplicate details and non-PDF documents.

**Sheet1** of `MDOT TTC Typ Cheat Sheet.xlsx` supplies titles, classifications, notes, and maintenance relationships for its original 82 construction typicals and 15 maintenance alternatives. The workbook is read by sheet name, not worksheet file order. Totals, sign quantities, saved usage counts, saved project selections, and the “DID NOT REVIEW” placeholder are not imported.

Workbook titles, notes, and original classifications are preserved in `filters`. Missing titles are filled from the matching MDOT catalog record; missing workbook classifications are “Unspecified.” Only case and whitespace variants are combined in those source fields. Original lane labels such as “2,” “2+,” and “4 or 5” remain available in the card's workbook disclosure. A maintenance sister is a related choice, not an automatic replacement or a recipient of the construction typical's RCOC classification.

The four filter sections are **Typical category** (Construction, Highway Maintenance, or Survey), **MDOT code**, **Roadway type**, and **Work activity**, in that order. There is no More filters section. Clear filters clears all four groups; Reset project restores the Construction category default and clears the other groups. Both collapse Possible matches.

**MDOT code** matches the segment immediately after the typical number, such as INT in `160-INT-LD-CLT-MID`. The current choices are AFAD, CLT, CLT(7), FW, GEN, INT, M, NFW, PULLOFF, S, SP, TR, and TS, each shown with its plain-language meaning. Options come from catalog identifiers, so a refresh can introduce new codes without hiding them. Multiple codes combine with OR, while other groups and search combine with AND; counts ignore choices in their own group. Later code segments do not match this filter: `4110A-M-TR-NFW-2L` matches M, while `4221-FW-EnR-O-LC-FREE` matches FW. A conflicting code also excludes a record from Possible matches. Code filtering does not infer or change roadway/work classifications, selections, or automatic Always sheets.

**Roadway type** offers Undivided, Divided, and Freeway in that order, followed by Unspecified. Each choice includes a short explanation: undivided roads have no median or barrier separating opposing traffic (a center turn lane remains undivided); divided roads separate opposing traffic; freeways control access through entrance and exit ramps. Freeway and Divided can overlap, consistent with [MDOT's road definitions](https://www.michigan.gov/mdot/About/mdot-road-terms-and-definitions). A sheet matches every roadway type explicitly included in its title, rather than treating these choices as mutually exclusive.

`roadway_classifications()` prefers the descriptive MDOT title, then the workbook title, then an explicit workbook roadway label. Two- or three-lane, two-way roadway titles establish Undivided applicability, including maintenance and survey sheets previously left Unspecified. Sheet 5401 matches all three types because its title and diagrams cover two-lane/two-way and divided/freeway roadways. Bare lane counts, two-way traffic alone, ramps, crossovers, and filename codes do not establish a roadway type. Non-freeway wording never supplies a Freeway match. Unclear applicability stays Unspecified, with an explanation to review the detail. These derived labels leave original workbook classifications intact and are regenerated on refresh.

General construction sheets **100–104** retain their **All roadway types** classification and original workbook values. They are required in every project, regardless of category or roadway. `selectable()` excludes these five records from search, available results, Possible matches, filter options, and filter counts. The complete catalog still contains 136 records; browsing covers the other 131 (83 construction, 32 maintenance, and 16 survey). GEN remains a code choice for the three optional general sheets 105–107.

**Work activity** offers **Close the right lane**, **Close the left lane**, **Close the center turn lane**, **Close multiple lanes**, **Work on the shoulder**, **Close a ramp**, and **Other work**. `workTaskInfo()` in `core.js` derives these browsing choices from verified closure, lane-position, and work-area classifications. Right/left follows traffic direction. A lane-closure record can match both a side and multiple lanes, counted once per result. Center-turn closures require explicit center-turn or CLTL wording in the MDOT/workbook title or work area; a center-of-road closure is not treated as a turn lane. Multiple-lane closures use explicit counts or multiple affected lane positions. Shoulder work excludes work outside the shoulder. A ramp treatment or operation does not establish a ramp closure.

Details outside those six tasks remain accessible through **Other work**, search, and clearing the task filter. This includes general sheets, shifts, intersections, mobile operations, and closures whose side is unspecified. Known lane closures with an unspecified side also appear as **Possible matches** when the selected task depends on that side, including multiple-lane closures with an unknown side. The same review applies when center-turn applicability or the number closed is not established. Known opposite-side closures, shifts without an established closure, and parking-lane-only closures do not become verified right/left closures. Original classifications remain on cards, and all descriptive labels remain searchable.

The underlying fields in `classifications` still separate **Roadway type**, **Traffic arrangement**, **Work area**, and **Control method**, so specific card labels and source classifications remain available. Roadway types are Divided, Undivided, and Freeway; arrangements include crossovers, closures, shifts, merges, and rolling roadblocks; methods include traffic regulators, temporary signals, and AFADs. `classify()` in the refresh script derives these only from explicit workbook labels and the record's workbook/MDOT titles. Filename abbreviations, notes about other typicals, and related sheets are not used to infer classifications. Unknown values remain Unspecified. These are browsing categories, not additional MDOT classifications.

A record can have multiple values: a freeway/divided-roadway detail with a closure and shift appears under both roadway types and both arrangements. OR within a field and AND across fields still apply, with no duplicate results; option counts can overlap. Intersection stays a Work area, while Crossovers, Single Shift, and Crush and Shape are removed from Work area. Notes, Signal Work, and Crush and Shape are not control methods. The cards retain their **Original workbook classifications** disclosure, including the workbook's Crush and Shape label on sheet 311, whose title instead identifies a crossover closure. Refreshes regenerate the browsing categories without changing the workbook, original labels, titles, notes, RCOC usage, or paint flags.

Typical category uses the display labels Construction, Highway Maintenance, and Survey while preserving the original workbook values. For typicals absent from the workbook, category comes from the MDOT source family; all other original workbook classifications, including RCOC usage and paint, remain “Unspecified.” Their cards identify the missing workbook classifications, while explicit titles can supply the browsing categories above. Four filename-only catalog entries (302, 303, 304, and 4204) use titles verified from the PDF title blocks, recorded in `DOCUMENT_TITLES` in the importer. Descriptive MDOT catalog titles take precedence if MDOT adds them later.

**Existing lanes** remains on detail cards and is searchable, but is not a filter. It describes the road before work; closure counts remain separate card information. Existing counts are labeled as totals across both directions (including a center turn lane where present), counts in the affected direction, or counts on the ramp. `lane_classifications()` prefers the descriptive MDOT title, falling back to the workbook title when MDOT lists only an identifier. Exact workbook counts can fill missing totals for explicitly undivided roads. Mixed labels and `+` values are preserved as source text, not interpreted as applicability ranges. For example, 123 uses MDOT's four-lane title while retaining the workbook's “4 or 5”; 202 uses three lanes in the affected direction while retaining “1+.”

The filter order is **Typical category → MDOT code → Roadway type → Work activity**, with every filter optional. All choices keep their live result counts, including zero counts. Lane counts remain visible on cards so users can compare details without an extra filter.

Closed-lane counts are separate from road size and shift counts, and include center turn lanes when explicitly closed. Staged closures such as 202 retain both one- and two-lane counts on the card and match Close multiple lanes without duplicating the typical. Parking-only and ramp-only closures are identified separately. Shoulder closures and work outside the shoulder show “No lane closure”; unspecified counts are never treated as zero. A shift, temporary signal, unnumbered group of lanes, or mobile operation alone does not establish the number closed. Shoulder references supply the Work on the shoulder task rather than lane-count labels. These are descriptive browsing categories; the original detail and workbook references remain available on every card.

**Lane position** is retained on cards as Left / inside, Right / outside, Center lane (including center turn lanes), Parking lane, or Unspecified; it is not a separate filter. `lane_positions()` uses affected lanes named in the descriptive MDOT title, falling back to the workbook work area when the title supplies no positions. Filename codes and the direction of a lane shift do not establish which lane is occupied. Multiple affected positions match multiple choices without duplicating a detail. Original inside/outside wording remains available in the workbook disclosure.

**Conditions noted by MDOT** appears on cards, related maintenance alternatives, and selected details. The repeatable `applicability_conditions()` importer extracts explicit duration, speed, traffic volume, sight distance, distance-from-vehicle, curb, reduced-width, advance-warning, and ramp-flow phrases from MDOT titles (or verified PDF titles when the listing is filename-only). It preserves qualifiers such as less than, longer than, maximum reduction, and or less; no speed thresholds or eligibility ranges are inferred. Empty conditions mean none were extracted from the title, not that the PDF has no additional requirements. Conditions remain visible information rather than filters, and the source PDF stays available for review.

The workbook's paint-quantity column supplies flags rather than a filter. “Yes” shows **Paint required** and “Maybe” shows **Paint may be required** on the typical and in the selected-details list. A reminder above the download buttons summarizes flagged selections and updates when details are added, removed, restored, or reset. “No” and “Unspecified” do not trigger a warning; missing values are not treated as a confirmed “No.”

PDF identifiers, URLs, and update dates are verified against the [MDOT construction catalog](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/TSSD/getSubCategoryDocuments.htm?category=Work+Zones&prjNumber=1403892&subCategory=Maintaining+Traffic+Typicals+), [maintenance catalog](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/TSSD/getSubCategoryDocuments.htm?category=Work+Zones&prjNumber=2173385&subCategory=Maintenance+Maintaining+Traffic+Typicals+), and [survey catalog](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/TSSD/getSubCategoryDocuments.htm?category=Work+Zones&prjNumber=2173386&subCategory=Survey+Maintaining+Traffic+Typicals+). Matching uses the unique typical number within its document family. For example, workbook 101 is mapped to `101-GEN-SPACING-CHARTS`, correcting its workbook hyperlink to 102. Codes with spacing or suffix discrepancies use the verified MDOT filename; literal plus signs in filenames are URL-encoded correctly.

`catalog.json` records the source workbook checksum, verification time, provenance rows, source URL and title, MDOT update date, PDF byte count, page count, and SHA-256 checksum. PDFs are stored under immutable checksum filenames. Source pages are copied without rasterization, resizing, or rotation changes. The downloaded packet adds a title page, index, and page-number labels; stored source PDFs stay intact.

## Refreshing the stored documents

The original `MDOT TTC Typ Cheat Sheet.xlsx` remains in the repository root. **Original Excel workbook (.xlsx)** beside the MDOT source library link downloads that preserved source file. Its checksum matches the workbook provenance recorded in `catalog.json`.

Use Python 3.10 or later with `pypdf` installed. From the repository root:

```powershell
py -3.13 calculators/mdot-ttc/update_catalog.py
```

An alternate workbook or output directory can be supplied with `--workbook` and `--output`. Review the changed catalog and run the checks below before publishing through the site's existing deployment process. Refresh is manual; the displayed verification date describes the stored snapshot, not a live check on MDOT.

The script validates the expected workbook headers, resolves every workbook reference, imports every individual PDF from all three MDOT listings, downloads PDFs into a temporary directory, checks signatures and page geometry, and only then promotes the files. It records the official family counts and replaces the catalog last, atomically. Missing catalogs or PDF links, ambiguous identifiers, invalid responses, and interrupted promotion cannot switch the active catalog to an incomplete set. Older PDF files are retained because an open browser may still reference an earlier catalog. The source workbook is never edited.

If an identifier in the previous stored catalog disappears from MDOT, refresh stops with the affected IDs. After confirming an official withdrawal, `--allow-removals` permits that change. Workbook references to withdrawn typicals must still be resolved in the workbook before refreshing.

## Project and export behavior

The **MDOT typical filename legend** sits between the filters and the Available/Selected panes and starts collapsed. It explains the entire filename: typical number, family/roadway codes, lane counts, positions, control methods, work and duration suffixes, and A/B variants. It includes worked examples for `152-CLT(7)-3(1R+2L)LC-2(L)SHIFT.pdf` and `160-INT-LD-CLT-MID`. Definitions come from MDOT's `100-GEN-KEY` and individual drawing titles; the source control previews sheet 100 inside the page. A/B suffixes are described as sheet-specific variants, not assigned a universal meaning. The legend is reference material and does not change filtering or project selections.

Every detail card has **Preview drawing**, including selected details, Always sheets, and Possible matches. Related maintenance alternatives have their own **Preview alternative** control. Click/tap the button, or activate it with Enter/Space, to open the panel. Hovering does not open or load a preview. **Previous/Next** visits every PDF page; **Zoom** offers Fit page and 150–300% of that fitted size, with scrolling for larger drawings. Close, Esc, or clicking outside dismisses the preview without changing the selection. Keyboard dismissal returns focus to the preview button. The panel stays inside the viewport and opens no browser window or tab.

`preview.js` loads the local PDF.js renderer and the requested stored PDF only when a preview is requested. It renders the actual PDF pages to a canvas, preserving their proportions and orientation, so refreshed PDFs need no separately generated thumbnails. Closing or switching previews cancels the old rendering task and releases its PDF/worker resources; stale responses cannot replace the current drawing. Failed or corrupt files identify the affected typical and offer **Retry preview**. The original View PDF link remains available. Previewing does not add cards or change downloads.

Filters sit above two independently scrolling panes: **Available details** on the left and **Selected details** on the right. Check cards to mark them, then use **Add marked →** or **← Remove marked** between the panes; each card also has an arrow for moving it individually. Marking alone does not change the project or its downloads. Both panes retain each detail's title, notes, flags, conditions, PDF links, and workbook classifications. Downloads and the project name are below the panes. On narrow screens the panes stack, with down/up transfer arrows between them.

Added cards leave the available pane. Removed cards return if they match the active filters. Available counts exclude optional details already in the report; filter counts still include them. Required sheets 100–104 are excluded from both. **Select all** in the available pane marks remaining exact matches; **Add marked** adds them. Possible matches require explicit review and marking. The selected pane’s **Select all** and **Remove marked** affect only user-added details, never the five required sheets. Checkboxes retain their checked, mixed, and disabled states. Marks are temporary and are cleared when their cards leave available results; project choices are saved independently. Selected and mobile totals include the five required sheets.

New and reset projects contain required sheets **100–104**, with no optional details chosen. **Construction** is the default category and shows 83 optional construction typicals. The category group starts expanded; the note explains RCOC construction projects use these speed-reduction provisions. **Clear filters** clears search and all groups to browse the 131 optional records, without changing the report. **Reset project** clears the name and optional choices, restores the Construction category, and collapses Always included; it preserves the five required sheets. Optional IDs and the project name are saved under `mdot-ttc-project-v1`. Older saved selections containing 100–104 are normalized into the required group without duplication or a missing-record warning. Removing every optional choice and reloading still leaves those five sheets. Storage failures leave the tool usable in memory with a visible message.

Filter options display live matching counts for optional details, including search and all other filter groups while ignoring their own group’s choices. Required sheets 100–104 never contribute to the counts. Zero-count options remain visible and selectable, and checked options can always be removed. Counts update without closing groups or moving keyboard focus. Adding an optional detail to the project does not change its filter counts.

**Possible matches — review detail** keeps potentially applicable sheets accessible when a selected classification is Unspecified. `possibleMatches()` requires the search and category to match and rejects every known conflict with an active filter. It lists only records missing at least one selected classification, with those fields named on the card. For example, construction sheet 205 appears here for Freeway + Close the right lane because its closure side is unspecified; the known left-only closure 204 does not. Missing lane counts do not restrict results or make a detail a possible match. Choosing a task that is established on the record, such as Work on the shoulder for 205, makes it an ordinary filter match. Main and possible results are disjoint and naturally sorted. The separate section starts collapsed; users can review a PDF and add a possible match individually, or explicitly mark reviewed cards and use Add marked. **Select all**, the main result total, and facet counts continue using exact classification matches. Changing or clearing filters preserves selections, and possible matches use the same saved IDs and exports as any manually chosen detail.

RCOC usage remains a card tag. Required inclusion is explicitly based on typical numbers **100–104**, independent of workbook RCOC values. These sheets always appear in the **Always included** disclosure in Selected details. It starts collapsed on page load and Reset project; users may expand it to read notes, preview drawings, or open the PDFs. There are no marking checkboxes or removal arrows for required sheets. Word, PDF, and ZIP use the same naturally sorted union and include each required sheet exactly once, even when no optional detail is chosen. Catalog validation rejects a catalog missing any required sheet, preventing an incomplete report.

Every export captures its full report before starting, including the Always sheets; PDF and ZIP exports also capture the project name. All formats use the same natural typical-number order. PDF and ZIP exports check each file's checksum and expected page count, abort the entire download on failure, and identify the affected typical. Retry uses the failed export format with the current selection and project name. A failed Always sheet must be restored before the report can be downloaded.

The combined PDF is for GI and the preliminary spec book. The project name is required for PDF downloads and may contain up to 160 characters. The PDF begins with a letter-sized title page and an index listing each typical number, title, and packet page range. Index rows link to the first page of that detail. Index pages expand as needed and repeat their column headings. Continuous “Page X of Y” labels include the title and index, so references match a PDF viewer's page count. Number labels occupy the blank bottom margin on current MDOT sheets, leaving their dimensions and original artwork intact. Project names use the PDF's standard Helvetica font; unsupported characters produce a clear error instead of being silently dropped.

The three download buttons have matching dimensions and a description explaining each file's use. **Download Word table** is the sole table export, for Maintenance of Traffic (MOT). It creates a real OOXML `.docx` table with Typical Number and Title columns, repeating headers, explicit column widths, wrapped titles, and rows that do not split across pages. Word downloads do not require a project name. There is no clipboard, table preview, or manual-copy control. Exports are available for a report containing only the five required sheets; the combined PDF still requires a project name. Export buttons are disabled while a download is being prepared or the catalog is unavailable.

**Download ZIP package** is for final submittal to MDOT Specs and Estimates through ProjectWise. It packages one original PDF per report detail in a flat archive, using the official typical identifiers as filenames (for example, `110-TR-NFW-2L.pdf`). The PDF bytes are unchanged, without the combined packet's title page, index, or added page numbers. The ZIP uses the same selected details and automatic Always sheets as the Word table. To include the entire library, choose **Clear filters**, check **Select all**, use **Add marked**, then download the ZIP. A project name is optional: when present, it supplies a safe filename prefix; otherwise the archive is named `mdot-ttc-details.zip`. This is a local download for submission through your existing ProjectWise process.

The export libraries are stored locally in `vendor/`: **pdf-lib 1.17.1**, **docx 9.5.1**, and [**fflate 0.8.3**](https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate), with their MIT licenses. Drawing previews use the compatibility build of [**PDF.js 6.3.289**](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/releases/tag/v6.3.289), with its Apache-2.0 license, worker, character maps, standard fonts, and image decoders stored in `vendor/pdfjs/`. Font/decoder licenses are included beside those files. The scripting sandbox is not included or enabled. `vendor/manifest.json` records the npm archive URLs, registry integrity hashes, and checksums for every local renderer asset as well as the export bundles. No CDN, package installation, or build step is needed to serve the selector.

## Validation

```powershell
node --test tests/*.test.cjs
py -3.13 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_mdot_ttc_catalog.py
py -3.13 tests/mdot_ttc_browser.py
py -3.13 tests/mdot_ttc_zip_browser.py
py -3.13 tests/mdot_ttc_transfer_browser.py
py -3.13 tests/mdot_ttc_preview_browser.py
py -3.13 tests/calculator_routes_browser.py
```

Core checks cover filters, notes, defaults, project-name and selection persistence, immutable export selections, escaping, source checksums, PDF page geometry, rotated pages, and export failures. Importer tests simulate failed downloads, malformed PDFs, ambiguous catalog records, and a failed final promotion. Browser checks cover the real downloads, title and index content, index destinations, page numbers, storage failures, catalog loading failures, mobile layout, and hosting under `/calc/`. ZIP browser checks independently read the archive with Python's `zipfile`, compare every file byte for all 136 typicals, check report order and Word-table agreement, and exercise optional names, frozen exports, missing/corrupt files, and retry behavior. Transfer browser checks cover marking and individual moves in both directions, possible matches, automatic sheets, keyboard use, independent scrolling, mobile navigation, persistence, and export agreement under root and `/calc/` hosting. Preview browser checks render all 150 pages, check proportions and visible drawing content, verify that hovering does not load a preview, exercise click/keyboard/touch, page controls, zoom, selected/Always/related cards, failure/retry, cancellation, and lazy loading at root and `/calc/`. Generated QA files stay in the system temporary directory.
