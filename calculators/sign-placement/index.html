Equations and letter-height check

Speed conversionv = V × (5280 / 3600)V in mph; v in ft/s.

Response distanceDr = vi × tvi is approach speed; t is response time in seconds.

Braking distanceDb = (vi² − vf²) / (2a)vf is target speed; a is positive deceleration in ft/s².

Sign placement estimateD = max(0, Dr + Db − L)L is the legibility distance before the sign, in feet.
