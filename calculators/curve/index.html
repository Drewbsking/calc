Freeway vs. expressway

Freeway: A divided highway with full access control. Traffic enters and leaves by ramps; there are no at-grade intersections or driveways.

Expressway: A divided highway with partial access control. It may have at-grade intersections or limited direct access.

3 Place three map points

Follow the road centerline: 1. Start of curve → 2. Midpoint → 3. End of curve.

Tap the map to place three points on the road centerline.
  • Select the Point of Curvature (PC), midpoint on curve, and Point of Tangent (PT).
  • Drag a marker to refine; red tangent lines should align with the centerline.
  • Target 30–120° separation on the constant-radius portion.
  • Only use with circular curves (avoid spirals).
  • Enter the advisory speed from an engineering study. The separate MDOT design-equation estimate uses measured full superelevation only.
  • Mobile: pinch to zoom. Desktop: Shift+drag for box zoom.
