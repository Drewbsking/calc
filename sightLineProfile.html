How the comparison works

The sight line uses Z(x) = Zeye + (Ztarget − Zeye) × x / L. Ground segments connect your measured points. Shading stops at the calculated crossings of each ground segment and the sight line.

Results cover the entered points and the straight segments between them. Unmeasured ground, vegetation, and other objects are not represented; this plot does not establish that the full sight path is clear.
