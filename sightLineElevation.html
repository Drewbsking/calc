Sight Line Elevation Checker

Find the elevation of a straight sight line at a distance measured from the eye point.

Sight line inputs

Use elevations on the same vertical datum. Include the eye and target heights above their ground elevations.

Enter total elevations, not just heights above the ground.

Initial values are an illustrative example. Results update as you type.

Elevation at the check point

Linear interpolation

Z(x) = Zeye + (Ztarget − Zeye) × x / L

x is the distance from the eye; L is the total distance. Use the same distance basis for both.

This tool interpolates between the two endpoint elevations. Use the ground profile tool to compare the line with measured ground points.
