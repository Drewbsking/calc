How the quantity is counted

Per curve: round up (length ÷ curve spacing) + 1 for the other endpoint + 6 end markers. Add the curves for the project total.

PC and PT are each counted once. The last interval may be shorter than the selected spacing. Shared markers and overlapping treatments between nearby curves are not deducted automatically.

Per-curve quantities
Each entered curve is counted once. “Ends” means three individual markers before PC and three after PT. Spacing is in feet.

Only the speed differences listed in the workbook are selectable. Each station counts as one physical marker, including PC and PT. The last curve interval may be shorter than the selected spacing.

The estimate follows the “Existing RCOC unwritten” column in RPM Spacing.xlsx, Sheet1!Q26:R33. The adjacent “Andy proposed” column and archived curvature guidance are separate methods and do not affect these totals.

Workbook placement note: Sheet1!Q34 calls for three RPMs before PC and after PT. The estimate places them at one, two, and three approach-spacing intervals outside each end. Curve markers at PC and PT are included separately.

Spacing stays constant within the curve, with a step to the approach spacing. The workbook’s separate five-second tangent calculation is not added to the three-marker allowance. The optional five-second advance treatment for supplemental centerline RPMs is described in MUTCD §3B.16 ¶04.
